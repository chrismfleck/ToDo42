Save 4 Two

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Save4Two is a shared list for two people. This page explains what the iOS app and save4two.com store, and what they do not.

What the app stores

When you use Save4Two, the list lives on your iPhone and, if you pair, in your iCloud so both phones can share it. That can include:

  • Item titles, notes, links, categories, and done or heart marks
  • Photos you add or share into an item
  • The first names you type for pairing, and the invite code used to connect the two phones

Sharing from Instagram, TikTok, Safari, or another app only saves what you choose to add: usually a title, a link, notes, and a photo if one is available.

Where it is stored

List data is stored on the device and in Apple iCloud (CloudKit) under your Apple Account. We do not run our own user database. Apple’s iCloud rules also apply: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Photos stay in the item you attach them to. The app can use your photo library only when you pick a picture. It does not scan your library.

The website

If you join the waitlist or write to support, we use the email you send only to reply or to tell you about TestFlight or the App Store launch. We do not sell that email.

What we do not do

Save4Two does not show ads, does not sell your data, and does not use tracking or analytics SDKs. We do not sell, rent, or share your list with other companies for their marketing. We do not require a separate social login.

Who can see a list

Only the two phones that pair with the same invite code, using iCloud. Unpairing stops new sync for that pair. Deleting an item or a photo removes it from the list on both phones after they sync. Deleting the app removes the copy on that phone. Data in iCloud stays until you remove it in the app or through your Apple Account / iCloud settings.

To ask us to remove a waitlist email, write to support@save4two.com.

Children

Save4Two is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
