## Description
See it. Save it. Do it together.

That sailboat trip, the cabin on the lake, the restaurant you both loved — stop losing ideas in DMs. Save 4 Two keeps one shared list of places, eats, and adventures.

Share a post from Instagram, TikTok, or Safari. Add a stay, a recipe, or a night out by hand. Sort it on Places, Fun, or Eats so the list stays a living wish book, not a junk drawer.

You and your partner see the same list. Heart what you want. Check it done. Add a photo so the memory stays.

New installs open with a short How to use page, then a few sample items you can keep or delete.

How you use it
• Spot it — a Reel, a cabin listing, a restaurant site. Share it into Save 4 Two.
• Sort it — Places, Fun, or Eats. Change the order, edit the photo and notes.
• Pick it together — heart it, do it, check it complete, keep the photo.

Pair two iPhones with iCloud. No extra account.
